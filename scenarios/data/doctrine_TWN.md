# Revealed Military Doctrine — Taiwan (Republic of China)

Taiwan's doctrine is **asymmetric denial under the Overall Defense Concept (ODC)** — reject parity with PLAN/PLAAF, embrace cost imposition, survive long enough for US intervention. The doctrine is explicit: Taiwan does not plan to defeat the PRC militarily; it plans to make an amphibious invasion prohibitively costly in the first 72 hours and to preserve enough survivable combat power and civil resilience that US intervention can decide the outcome.

The ODC was articulated by former Chief of the General Staff Adm. Lee Hsi-min (2017–2019) and represents a structural pivot away from symmetric force structure (legacy fighter buys, tank brigades) toward asymmetric denial (mobile anti-ship missiles, sea mines, mobile SAMs, distributed coastal defense). The pivot is incomplete — institutional resistance from the ROC services has slowed reform — but it is the declared doctrinal direction.

## Revealed Patterns

1. **Porcupine strategy is the organizing concept.** Make the first 72 hours of an amphibious landing so costly that PLA either fails or US has time to flow forces. Key systems: AGM-84 Harpoon (land-based coastal defense, ~400 missiles procured 2020), HF-3 supersonic ASCM (indigenous), Sea Dragon sea mines, mobile MIM-104 Patriot SAMs, MIM-23 Hawk SAMs, F-16V air-to-air. The doctrine accepts that the ROCAF cannot win an air superiority contest with PLAAF; it instead disperses and preserves jets for sortie generation.

2. **Defensive identity is psychologically constraining.** Taiwan does not strike first. This is not just policy — it is identity. The narrative ("PRC attacked; Taiwan defended") is a strategic asset on which international sympathy and US intervention authority depend. Losing the moral high ground is treated as nearly equivalent to losing the war. Pre-emptive strike on PRC mainland is *not* in Taiwan's plan even if militarily advantageous.

3. **Decentralized, survivable command and control.** ODC assumes early PRC decapitation strike (missile + cyber + SOF) targeting Taipei C2 nodes. Plan: distributed command posts, hardened sites in the Central Mountain Range, automated mission orders pre-positioned at unit level so subordinate commanders can fight without national-level direction for the opening phase. Han Kuang exercises (annual) test this.

4. **Reserve mobilization reform 2022+.** Conscription extended from 4 months to 12 months effective 2024 generation. US-style basic training (combat marksmanship, urban warfare, drone, medical) replacing the historical "haircut and floor mopping" reservist culture. Full mobilization timeline is 48–72 hours and is highly visible — covert mobilization is impossible, which creates a signaling dilemma (mobilize too early and provoke; too late and be caught unprepared).

5. **Coastal defense over blue-water navy.** ROCN cannot match PLAN tonnage. Modernization investment goes into Tuo Chiang-class corvettes (small, fast, ASCM-armed), mine warfare, and indigenous Hai Kun-class diesel submarine program (first hull launched 2023). Surface fleet (Kang Ding, Cheng Kung frigates) is for peacetime presence, not wartime decisive engagement.

6. **Civil defense and societal resilience.** ODC explicitly includes civil resistance: shelters (Taipei has the highest shelter density per capita in the world), alternative power generation, food and fuel reserves (~90 days petroleum, ~8 days LNG), and population-level resilience training. Pattern: Taiwan plans for occupation/blockade scenarios, not just battlefield outcomes.

7. **Threat absorption rather than matching.** Median line crossings 2020–2022: PLA air incursions normalized, Taiwan shifted from full intercept-scrambles to track-and-monitor to reduce pilot fatigue and airframe wear. Pattern: Taiwan manages escalation by absorbing rather than matching provocations. Critics argue this looks like acquiescence; the doctrine accepts that risk to preserve forces and avoid handing PRC a provocation pretext.

8. **US dependency channel for intel + arms employment doctrine.** Taiwan receives US intelligence products, US-supplied weapons require US approval for employment doctrine (e.g., target sets, ROE on Harpoon employment), and Taiwan's defense plans are coordinated informally with USINDOPACOM. Acting without US knowledge risks the security guarantee — but the channel also means Taiwan's operational autonomy is constrained.

9. **Han Kuang exercises as doctrine test bed.** Annual joint exercise (since 1984), increasingly realistic since 2022. Tests crisis-response from invasion warning through opposed-landing defense, including reserve mobilization, dispersed C2, urban defense, and civil-military coordination. Observable to PRC, which uses Han Kuang to read ROC posture and reform progress.

10. **Strict avoidance of provocation that gives PRC a casus belli.** Taiwan's hardest political constraint: any action that PRC can frame as "Taiwan declared independence" or "Taiwan provoked" undermines US intervention authority and unifies PRC domestic opinion. This eliminates many escalatory options (formal independence declaration, hosting US permanent bases, nuclear program) that might otherwise be available.

## Operational Tempo

**Reactive and defensive; never first-mover.** Tempo is set by PRC. Taiwan's planning assumes PRC chooses the timing; Taiwan responds. This places enormous weight on early warning (US intel sharing is critical) and on reserve mobilization timing. In the strategic competition phase (current), tempo is slow — measured diplomatic responses, arms procurement cycles. In a kinetic phase, tempo would compress to hours, but always reactively.

## Rules of Engagement

**Defensive only; restrictive on first use of force.** ROC military ROE require positive identification, proportionality, and authorization for kinetic engagement against PLA forces. Notable carve-outs: standing authority to fire on PLA forces *inside* ROC sovereign airspace/waters (12nm); standing authority to defend against direct attack. Outside those triggers, escalation authority runs through the President via the NSC.

## Anti-Patterns (Doctrinally Inconsistent)

- **First strike on PRC mainland.** Politically catastrophic — destroys the casus-belli narrative and US intervention authority. Not in any plan.
- **Formal declaration of independence.** Crosses PRC's stated red line and triggers war on PRC's terms.
- **Hosting US permanent bases.** Crosses PRC's second-tier red line and triggers same outcome.
- **Air superiority parity contest with PLAAF.** ROCAF doctrinally rejects this — preserves jets in dispersal for sortie generation, not for decisive air engagement.
- **Hosting nuclear weapons / pursuing weapons program.** US dismantled ROC nuclear R&D in 1988; no return.
- **Negotiated reunification on PRC terms.** Domestically untenable; Sunflower Movement 2014 demonstrated the public's hard constraint.
