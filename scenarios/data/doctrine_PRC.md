# Revealed Military Doctrine — People's Republic of China

PLA doctrine is **informatized joint operations, decisive shock, gray-zone preference below the threshold of armed response, and A2/AD-enabled regional denial.** The current generation of PLA doctrine descends from "Local Wars Under Informatized Conditions" (early 2000s) through "Intelligentized Warfare" (post-2015), emphasizing integrated cyber/EW/space/missile/conventional operations, with the explicit goal of breaking adversary command-and-control before kinetic engagement. The PLA has not fought a peer war since 1979, so much doctrine is theoretical — but it is rehearsed extensively through exercises and is closely observed.

The Taiwan contingency is the central organizing problem of PLA modernization since 1996. Force structure, training, and doctrine are explicitly shaped by the goal of (a) preventing US intervention, and (b) achieving political resolution faster than the US NSC can authorize a military response.

## Revealed Patterns

1. **Salami slicing — accumulate fait accompli below the response threshold.** South China Sea island construction (2013–2016): individually small steps — survey, dredge, build, militarize — each calibrated to be sub-threshold for US military response. When confronted, PRC publicly denied militarization until installation was complete, then declared the islands sovereign. Pattern: PRC prefers slow accumulation over single decisive moves, *unless* a window of opportunity appears.

2. **Gray zone preference: coast guard, maritime militia, fishing fleet, cyber, propaganda.** PRC Coast Guard, China Maritime Militia (sea militia operating from designated ports), and the People's Armed Forces Maritime Militia routinely operate at the margin of armed conflict. These forces are PLA-coordinated but legally civilian — usable for actions PLAN would not directly conduct. The Kinmen boarding pattern, Scarborough Shoal 2012, and Second Thomas Shoal 2023 actions are the template.

3. **Calibrated violence below firearm thresholds.** Galwan Valley 2020 (Sino-Indian border): lethal hand-to-hand combat (~20 Indian KIA, ~4 PRC KIA) but strict avoidance of firearms, preserving plausible deniability of "escalation." Pattern: PRC will accept and inflict casualties but engineers the violence to stay below the adversary's escalation doctrine threshold.

4. **A2/AD as the central PRC operational concept.** DF-21D and DF-26 anti-ship ballistic missiles, DF-17 hypersonic glide vehicle, integrated PLAAF and PLAN over-the-horizon ASCM coverage, and PLARF strike packages are designed to deny US carrier access to the Western Pacific within the First Island Chain in a Taiwan contingency. The 1996 humiliation (when US deployed two CSGs through the Strait and PRC could not respond) is the direct ancestor of the entire A2/AD investment program.

5. **Decisive shock doctrine for an opposed Taiwan contingency.** The PLA operational concept for Taiwan is *not* a slow blockade — it is a short, sharp, high-intensity campaign designed to achieve political resolution within 7–30 days, before US forces can flow into theater. Opening salvo expected: cyber strike on Taiwan C2, missile strike on Taiwan airbases and ports, SOF infiltration, then opposed amphibious. The timeline is gated by PLA confidence that the operation can complete before US intervention.

6. **Centralized C2 under CMC; Xi-direct decision authority.** Unlike the US NSC process, the Central Military Commission *is* the chain of command. Xi chairs it. Decisions can be made faster than in Washington, but single-leader psychology is load-bearing — there is no system that checks Xi's risk calculus. This creates both speed and brittleness: a fast decision can also be a fast wrong decision with no internal correction mechanism.

7. **Eastern Theater Command holds pre-authorized Taiwan plans.** ETC has plans for blockade (multiple variants — partial/full, naval/air/civilian), missile strike, and full opposed amphibious. Activation requires CMC order. Operational planning is well-developed; *joint execution* under contested conditions is untested.

8. **Propaganda coordination is a precondition for major action.** The Central Propaganda Department must pre-position domestic narrative before or simultaneously with military action. The 2022 Pelosi response was telegraphed for weeks; the 2024 Joint Sword exercises were preceded by media buildup. This means genuine *strategic surprise* (out-of-the-blue attack with no propaganda lead) is institutionally difficult for the PRC system.

9. **Risk aversion on nuclear escalation.** Official PRC nuclear policy is No First Use (NFU). PRC is the only NPT-recognized nuclear state with declared NFU. Nuclear modernization (estimated 500 warheads in 2024, projected ~1000 by 2030 per DoD CMPR) is expanding the arsenal but not the doctrine — the conventional-nuclear firebreak is something PRC has historically been reluctant to blur. NFU is a peacetime posture and is revisable under existential regime threat, but PRC behavior has never tested it.

10. **Untested at scale — institutional risk of catastrophic operational failure.** The PLA has not conducted a complex joint operation in actual combat. The 1979 Sino-Vietnamese War revealed serious deficiencies in PLA performance and was the original driver of the modernization program. The PLA Eastern Theater Command has exercised Taiwan scenarios extensively, but exercises do not test contested logistics, contested communications, opposed amphibious under fire, or sustained operations against a peer adversary. PLA leadership knows this. The asymmetry between paper capability and demonstrated capability is real and is a constraint on PRC risk acceptance.

## Operational Tempo

**Patient long-game punctuated by sudden decisive moves.** PRC strategic culture emphasizes *shi* (势) — the propensity of a situation — and waiting for the moment when "the situation is ripe." This produces years or decades of incremental positioning, followed by abrupt action when conditions are read as favorable. Pelosi response 2022 was launched within ~48 hours of her landing; South China Sea construction took 3 years; Hong Kong NSL was effectively instant once decided. The tempo is bimodal: glacial or blitz, rarely steady-state.

## Rules of Engagement

**Permissive in gray zone, restrictive in declared use of force.** PRC Coast Guard and Maritime Militia operate with broad latitude for harassment, ramming, water cannon, boarding. PLA naval and air units operate with significantly more discipline and clearly require CMC authorization for kinetic engagement. The two-tier ROE structure is deliberate: it allows political action below the threshold of declared force while preserving political control over actual military escalation.

## Anti-Patterns (Doctrinally Inconsistent)

- **First use of nuclear weapons.** Official NFU; deeply embedded.
- **Direct strike on US homeland.** Crosses the existential threshold for the US response and undermines any prospect of US restraint.
- **Mass-casualty conventional attack on Japanese civilian population.** Strategically self-defeating — guarantees JSDF combat involvement and triggers Article 5.
- **Opposed amphibious assault on Taiwan without prior C2 paralysis.** The PLA understands this would likely fail; it is reserved for the case where pre-strike has succeeded.
- **Public concession after assertive action.** Loss-of-face costs are existential for CCP legitimacy; PRC will absorb economic damage rather than back down publicly once committed.

## Action-Type Bias (qualitative)
